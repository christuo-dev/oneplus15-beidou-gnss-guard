Research, reproduction, ports, and improvements are welcome, but **attribution is
required when this work is referenced or adapted**.

If you use or reference the research conclusions, EFS paths, `0x4905/0x5905`
analysis, optional subscription-item handling, two-stage repair flow, GNSS HAL
reload method, source code, or module implementation in any public repository,
root module, article, tutorial, video, social post, or port, include a prominent
credit such as:

